MY TRACKER / ABOUT US

A clearer picture of every workday.

My Tracker helps people keep their own record of hours, estimated earnings, and pay periods across multiple workplaces.

Why we built My Tracker

Work schedules and pay periods can be hard to follow, especially when you work at more than one place. My Tracker puts shifts, hours, estimated gross earnings, and payment status in one simple view.

What you can do

  • Create separate workplaces with their own pay schedules and rate history.
  • Record shifts, unpaid breaks, and dates in a calendar.
  • Review weekly, biweekly, or monthly pay periods and mark them unpaid, partially paid, or fully paid.
  • Review reports and share a period summary without sharing gross amounts.

Designed around your own records

Each account starts with its own workplaces. My Tracker estimates earnings from the hourly rate saved for each shift. Your employer’s records and actual pay statement are the source to use for payroll questions.

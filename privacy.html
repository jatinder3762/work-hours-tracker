MY TRACKER / PRIVACY

Your work records deserve clear handling.

This page explains the information My Tracker uses to run your account and show your shifts. Last updated September 24, 2026.

Information you provide

When you sign up, the service processes your email address and password for authentication. When you use My Tracker, you can enter workplace names, optional company names and addresses, pay schedules, hourly rates, shift dates and times, breaks, payment amounts, and account view preferences.

How it is used

We use these details to sign you in, save and display your own records, calculate hours and estimated gross earnings, organize pay periods, and show reports. A period report is shared only when you choose its share action; that image omits gross earnings. If you send it through another app, that app handles the copy you share.

Where information goes

Account authentication and work data are handled through Supabase. The public website is hosted on GitHub Pages. The site also loads the Supabase JavaScript library from jsDelivr. These services may process technical information such as network requests and IP addresses to deliver the app.

Supabase database policies are configured to limit each signed-in user to their own workplace data. No internet service can promise absolute security.

Information on your device

Your browser may retain a sign-in session and a timestamp used to limit repeated signup attempts. A service worker may cache the app’s files so the interface loads more reliably. Shared images saved or sent from your device are under your control.

Your choices and requests

You can edit workplaces and shifts within the app, subject to paid-period locks, and sign out at any time. The app does not currently provide a self-service account deletion or full data export screen. For a privacy or deletion request, ask the maintainer through a general project issue to arrange a private contact method; do not include personal details in a public issue.

Retention and deletion timing depends on the service configuration and the outcome of a request. This page will be updated when a private support channel or self-service controls are available.

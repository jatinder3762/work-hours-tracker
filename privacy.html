MY TRACKER / PRIVACY

Your privacy matters.

A short overview of how My Tracker uses your information. Last updated September 24, 2026.

Information and purpose

My Tracker uses your email to manage your account. It saves the workplace, shift, hourly rate, and payment details you enter so you can view your hours, estimated earnings, and pay periods.

Services and sharing

Account and work records are stored through Supabase; the website is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads the Supabase library from jsDelivr. These services may receive technical information needed to deliver the app. Your work records are shown in your account. A period image is shared only when you choose to share it, and it leaves out gross earnings. Anyone you send an image to can see the work details it contains.

Your choices

You can edit most records in the app, subject to paid-period locks. The app does not yet have an account deletion or full export screen. Use the mail icon below to ask about your data or request deletion. Never send a password or recovery link.

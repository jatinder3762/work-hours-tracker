Use of the app

My Tracker is for keeping your own work records. Enter accurate information, protect your sign-in details, and do not try to access someone else’s account or disrupt the service.

Earnings are estimates

Hours and gross earnings depend on what you enter. They may differ from actual pay because of deductions, premiums, and adjustments. Use your employer’s pay statement for official amounts. Features and availability may change; use the mail icon below for questions.

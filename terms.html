MY TRACKER / TERMS

Simple terms for using My Tracker.

Please read these terms before creating an account. Last updated September 24, 2026.

Using the service

My Tracker is a personal record-keeping tool for shifts, hours, rate history, and payment status. You are responsible for entering accurate information and keeping your account credentials private. Use the service lawfully and do not attempt to access another person’s records or disrupt the app.

Estimates and actual pay

Hours, earnings, and partial-payment progress are calculated from the information you enter. Estimated gross earnings do not account for taxes, deductions, premiums, tips, or employer adjustments. My Tracker is not a payroll provider, time-clock verification system, or source of legal, tax, or employment advice. Check actual pay and entitlements against your employer’s records and official pay statement.

Your content and sharing

You keep responsibility for the work information you enter. The app uses it to provide your account features. If you choose to share a period summary, you decide who receives it; shared images omit gross earnings but can still reveal dates, hours, and workplace information.

Availability and changes

The app may change as features are improved and may occasionally be unavailable. Review important records independently before relying on them for pay disputes or other consequential decisions. Material updates to these terms will be reflected here with a revised date.

Questions

For general product questions, open a project issue. Issues are public, so never include passwords, recovery links, or private account details.

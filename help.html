Short answers to the questions that come up when logging shifts and reviewing pay.

Getting started

  1. Create an account and confirm your email if asked.
  2. Select Add workplace, enter the first pay-period date, pay frequency, and an hourly rate if you know it.
  3. Open the workplace calendar, choose a day, then enter start and end times and any unpaid break.

You can add shifts from the workplace start date through today. Future dates and fully paid periods cannot accept new shifts.

Why are earnings blank?

A shift needs an hourly rate effective on or before its date. In the workplace, select Add hourly rate. If old shifts were saved without a rate, choose an effective date that covers them and select the option to apply the rate to unrated shifts. Existing recorded rates and locked paid shifts are preserved.

Pay periods and partial payments

Select Pay periods to review a period. A partial payment records the amount received and estimates covered versus pending hours; the period stays open. Mark it fully paid once you have confirmed all hours were paid. A fully paid period locks its shifts until you unlock it with your account password.

The covered-hours figure is an estimate based on recorded gross rates; deductions can change the actual amount received.

Account and sharing

Use Account when signed in to change your password. If you cannot sign in, choose Forgot password? from the sign-in form and follow the email link. You can share a period image from the pay period view; the shared report leaves out gross earnings.
